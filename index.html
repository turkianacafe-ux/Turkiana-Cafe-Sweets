About

At Turkiana Café & Sweets, we bring a taste of Turkey to everyone. We specialize in traditional Turkish sweets like baklava, kunefe, and Turkish delight, all made with high-quality ingredients and authentic recipes. But it’s not just about sweets — we also serve a full café menu. Our guests can enjoy Turkish coffee, espresso-based drinks, teas, fresh juices, and even mojitos. For food, we offer breakfast items, sandwiches, salads, and burgers, so there’s something for everyone. We focus on authenticity and quality. Every dessert is crafted carefully to reflect Turkish culinary traditions, and many of our sweets are beautifully packaged so they can also be given as gifts. With multiple branches in Qatar, including a cozy spot in Msheireb Downtown, we aim to give every customer a warm, welcoming experience — whether they’re stopping by for a quick coffee, a meal, or some special treats to take home. In short, Turkiana is our space where Turkish culture, flavors, and hospitality come together in every bite and sip./p>
